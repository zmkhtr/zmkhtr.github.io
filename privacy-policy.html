Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At Khatm, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use our mobile apps (iOS and Android) or our browser extension. Your reading progress, settings and blocking choices stay on your device. We never collect your name, email address, contacts, location or the list of apps and websites you block. To understand how Khatm is used and to fix crashes, we collect anonymous usage and crash data, which you can turn off at any time.

Information Stored on Your Device

The following information is stored locally on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Reading Progress: Your current verse, bookmarks and reading history
  • App Settings: The apps or websites you block, your unlock goal and your feature preferences
  • Language Preferences: Your selected translation, script and reciter
  • Recite to Unlock: The speech recognition model you download

Anonymous Usage & Crash Data

Unless you turn it off, Khatm sends anonymous usage and crash data to help us improve the app. We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics in the mobile apps, and Google Analytics in the browser extension. This data includes:

  • Feature usage: For example, when a verse is read or changed, when a reciter is chosen, when an unlock goal is set or reached, and which settings are changed
  • Counts and settings values: Verse numbers, reciter IDs, the number of blocked apps or websites (never which ones), and your chosen language or script
  • Crash and error reports: Technical details of crashes and errors, such as the screen you were on and the app version
  • Device information: Device model, operating system and app version, an approximate country or region derived from your IP address, and a random identifier for this installation

This data is not linked to your identity. We do not use an advertising identifier, we do not track you across other apps or websites, we do not sell this data, and we do not use it for advertising. It never includes your name, email address, the websites you visit, or anything you type. Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase privacy terms and keeps it for a limited period (up to 14 months for analytics and 90 days for crash reports).

How to turn it off: Open Settings in the app or extension and switch off "Share anonymous usage & crash data". Collection stops immediately.

Recite to Unlock & Microphone

When you use Recite to Unlock, Khatm uses your microphone to follow your recitation. Your voice is processed entirely on your device by the downloaded speech model. Audio is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The microphone is only used while Recite to Unlock is on.

Quran.com Sync (Optional)

If you choose to sign in with your Quran.com (Quran Foundation) account, Khatm syncs your reading progress and bookmarks with that account. This data is sent to Quran Foundation and handled under their privacy policy. Khatm only stores the sign-in token on your device. You can use Khatm without signing in.

Permissions

Khatm requires certain permissions to function properly:

  • Screen Time (iOS) / Usage Access & Overlay (Android) / Browser Permissions (Extension): To block the apps or websites you choose and show a verse instead
  • Microphone (optional): For Recite to Unlock, processed on your device
  • Notifications (optional): For reading reminders
  • Storage: To save your reading progress and preferences
  • Internet: To download Quran content, audio, translations and the recitation model, to sync with Quran.com if you sign in, and to send anonymous usage and crash data unless you turn it off

Your Data Control

  • Turn off anonymous usage and crash data in Settings at any time
  • Use Khatm without signing in to Quran.com
  • Clear the app's data through your device's settings
  • Uninstall the app or extension to remove all data stored on your device
  • Contact us to ask about or request deletion of analytics data linked to your installation

Delete Your Data

Khatm does not have user accounts, and the usage and crash data we receive is not linked to your name or email. To delete your Khatm data:

  1. Stop collection: Open Settings in the app or extension and switch off "Share anonymous usage & crash data". Nothing more is sent from that moment.
  2. Delete data on your device: Clear the app's data in your device settings, or uninstall the app or extension. This removes your reading progress, settings, the downloaded speech model and the random installation identifier.
  3. Request deletion of data already sent: Email appkhatm@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and tell us your platform (iOS, Android or Chrome extension), your device model and roughly when you used Khatm. We will delete the matching analytics and crash data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted: usage events, crash and error reports, and device information sent to Google Firebase or Google Analytics for your installation. What is kept: nothing beyond the retention periods above. Even without a request, analytics data is deleted automatically after 14 months and crash reports after 90 days.

Quran.com account: Khatm does not create or store Quran.com accounts. To delete your Quran.com account or the reading progress synced to it, use your account settings on Quran.com or contact Quran Foundation. Signing out of Quran.com in Khatm removes the sign-in token from your device.

Children's Privacy

Khatm does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The anonymous usage data described above does not identify anyone.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
